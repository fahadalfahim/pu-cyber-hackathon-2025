About the Hackathon

PU Cyber Security Hackathon 2025 is a club-based event focused on modern cyber security challenges. Participants will design intelligent systems, explore data privacy, and work in teams to build creative technical solutions.

Theme

Cyber Security & Data Privacy driven problems, focused on practical scenarios.

Format

Team-based hackathon with coding, system design and presentation rounds.

Who can join?

Primarily Presidency University students interested in security, ML, or software.
